What we heard

The brief

A webcam eye-gaze communication tool for people who have lost speech. It must feel warm, dignified and calm, never clinical, and read clearly at app-icon and favicon size.

warmdignifiedcalmhumanaccessible

Success: unlike the usual blue medical or eye-icon marks, recognisable at 24 px, works in one colour and reversed, says 'seeing becomes saying' in one glance.

VisualEyes — final identity2
Final design

Eye Bubble

An eye that is also a speech bubble: seeing becomes saying.

  • One idea in one glance: the product is gaze turned into voice.
  • The warm coral pupil is the point of gaze, the one thing the user chooses.
  • Avoids the usual blue-and-clinical look of assistive tech: teal and coral feel human.
  • Reversed tile version stays readable at 16 px where the full-colour ring would blur.
