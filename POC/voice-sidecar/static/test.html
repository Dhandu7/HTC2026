VocalEyes voice lab

Record your own voice, build it on this computer, and hear it speak. Nothing leaves this machine. checking engine…

1. Consent

A voice can only be built with the voice owner's consent. Recordings and the built voice are stored on this computer only, and you can export or permanently delete them at the bottom of this page.

2. Record or upload clips

Aim for 10 to 20 clips of 5 to 15 seconds each, in a quiet room, speaking naturally, about 1 to 5 minutes in total. You can also upload WAV files you already have.

3. Build the voice

4. Test it

AI-assisted speech. If it does not sound like you, add more varied clips, try 20 to 30 seconds of calm natural speech, or change the voice strength.
