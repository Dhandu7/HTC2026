words-30k.txt: the 30,000 most frequent English words, most common first, one per line.

Generated on 8 November 2026 from the wordfreq project by Robyn Speer
(https://github.com/rspeer/wordfreq), English list, "best" frequencies, using
wordfreq.top_n_list("en", ...). Tokens that are not plain words (digits, symbols,
single letters other than "a" and "i") were dropped, and the app's slur filter
(src/safety/slurs.ts) removed 13 entries.

Licence of the data: Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
(https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/). Attribution: Robyn Speer, wordfreq.
wordfreq's English frequencies combine several sources, which it credits in its own
documentation, including SUBTLEX (Brysbaert et al.; freely available data), OpenSubtitles,
Google Books Ngrams, Wikipedia, Twitter and the Leeds Internet Corpus.

This file replaces the earlier "Google 10k English" list, which was for research and
educational use only. On the repo's held-out persona sentences (scripts/eval-suggestions.ts
--local-only) the new list needs fewer selections per character for every persona.
